When to start

Start change management at project kick-off — not at go-live. The earlier you start, the smoother the landing.

What is change management? It is the structured approach to moving people from their current way of working to a new one — with less resistance, fewer mistakes, and faster adoption. Without it, even the best technology or process change can fail.

Tips for non-experts

Over-communicatePeople fear what they don't know. Send updates even when nothing has changed.
Find championsOne enthusiastic adopter per team is worth ten training sessions.
Measure adoptionTrack usage, not just attendance. Training does not equal change.
Adapt the planYour plan will change. That is normal. Update it openly with the team.

Common mistakes to avoid

Starting change management at go-live — it is too late. Start at kick-off.

Treating training as the only change activity.

Not involving impacted users until the solution is already built.

Assuming leadership support means frontline buy-in.
